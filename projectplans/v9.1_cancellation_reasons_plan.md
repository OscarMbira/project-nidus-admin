# v9.1 — Customer Cancellation Reason Capture

**Status:** Complete  
**Created:** 2026-07-01  
**Parent feature:** v9.0 — 30-Day Refund CRUD  
**Feature:** Allow customers to submit structured cancellation reasons when cancelling a subscription. Admins see reason analytics and can manage the reason category library.

---

## Background & Scope

When a customer cancels their subscription, we lose the opportunity to understand why — and to act on it. This plan adds:

- A **reason category library** that admins manage (`admin.cancellation_reason_categories`)
- A **customer-facing cancellation modal** in Platform and Simulator landing apps that captures:
  - A predefined reason (from the category library)
  - Optional free-text elaboration
- A **cancellation requests table** (`public.cancellation_requests`) storing each submission
- An **admin view** in the Subscriptions module: list of all submissions, reason trend counts, and category management
- A **sidebar link** under "Subscription & Billing" for easy access

### What this is NOT

- This does not replace or trigger the refund flow — it sits alongside it. Admins can cross-reference a cancellation reason when reviewing a refund, but the two are separate records.
- It does not block the cancellation action. The customer still cancels; the modal just asks "before you go — why?"
